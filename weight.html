How to use this stones and pounds converter

Enter stones and pounds on the left to convert to kilograms, or enter kilograms on the right to convert back to stones and pounds. The result updates immediately, so it is useful when filling out forms, checking a fitness app value or translating a UK body-weight measurement into metric units.

You can use whole stones, decimal pounds or decimal kilograms. The converter is a unit tool only and does not assess whether a body weight is healthy or appropriate.

Why the UK still uses stones

In everyday life, many people in the UK still describe body weight using stones and pounds. In healthcare, fitness apps and international contexts, the same value is usually recorded in kilograms.

This converter helps switch between the two systems quickly without rounding surprises. It is designed for conversion only, not health assessment or medical advice.
