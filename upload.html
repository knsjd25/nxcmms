What not to upload

Only upload images you are allowed to share through a public or semi-public link. Do not use this service for private, illegal, harmful or rights-infringing content.

  • ID documents, passports, bank cards, financial documents, private screenshots or sensitive personal records.
  • Illegal, adult, sexually explicit, violent, hateful, abusive or exploitative content.
  • Copyrighted images, brand assets or artwork that you do not have permission to upload.
  • Malware-related content, phishing, scam screenshots, deceptive content or images used to support fraud.
  • Unsafe or exploitative images involving minors, schools or private family situations.
  • Private screenshots, confidential work files or content that should not be accessible by link.

How uploaded images are reviewed

Mini-Tools.uk operates image hosting with manual review and automated safeguards. This helps reduce abuse, copyright problems, and unsafe uploads while keeping the service useful for everyday sharing.

  • New uploads may enter a review queue before or after a hosted link is created, depending on retention type, risk signals and service settings.
  • Images that appear to violate upload rules, privacy, copyright, safety, or applicable law may be rejected, hidden, or removed.
  • Repeat or high-risk upload sources may be limited or blocked to protect other users and the service.
  • Approved long-term storage codes are intended for invited or pre-approved use cases and may receive additional review.

FAQ

Is this image hosting tool free to use?

Yes. You can upload images and copy direct links, Markdown, HTML or BBCode snippets for common publishing workflows.

How long are uploaded images kept?

Uploaded images may be stored for sharing and display. Do not upload private, sensitive or illegal content.

What is the long-term storage code for?

It is an optional code for approved long-term image hosting. Normal free image uploads can leave it blank.

Can I use the link in a GitHub README?

Yes. Upload the image, copy the Markdown output, and paste it into README.md or a GitHub issue comment.

Which output format should I copy?

Use Direct URL for plain links, Markdown for README files and docs, HTML for web pages, and BBCode for forums.

Are uploaded images reviewed by a person?

Yes. The service uses manual review for hosted uploads, along with upload rules, retention settings and removal requests. Do not upload content you are not allowed to share.

What is an online image upload tool?

It sends a selected image to a hosting service and returns a URL that can be copied and shared.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required for normal uploads. Approved permanent storage uses a separate storage code.

Can I get a shareable image link?

Yes. A successful upload provides a direct URL plus Markdown, HTML and BBCode outputs.

Can I use the image link on forums or messages?

Yes, when the destination accepts direct URLs or BBCode and you have permission to share the image.

What image formats are supported?

The uploader accepts JPG, PNG, GIF and WebP images up to 5 MB.
