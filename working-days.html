How this UK working days calculator works

This tool counts every calendar day between your start date and end date, then removes Saturdays and Sundays. If bank holiday exclusion is enabled, it also removes the built-in 2026 and 2027 bank holidays for the selected UK region.

England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland do not always share the same bank holiday calendar, so the region setting matters. This is useful when planning project timelines, invoice due dates, annual leave cover, delivery schedules or internal service-level targets.

How to use it

  • Choose the start and end date for the period you want to check.
  • Select England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland.
  • Keep “Exclude bank holidays” enabled if you want a business-day style count.
  • Read the working-day total, calendar-day total, weekend count and excluded bank holidays.

Who this is useful for

The calculator is useful for UK small businesses, contractors, project managers, operations teams, students and anyone planning around weekdays rather than raw calendar days. It helps answer practical questions such as how many working days are left before a deadline or how long a project window is after weekends and bank holidays.

Important limits

This page is a planning tool, not a payroll, employment law or legal deadline system. Some organisations treat bank holidays, part-time working patterns, local holidays and contractual deadlines differently. For formal HR, payroll, court, tax or contract deadlines, check the relevant rules before relying on the result.

What counts as a working day?

In this calculator, a working day is a Monday to Friday that is not excluded as a bank holiday for the selected UK region.

Does it include both the start date and end date?

Yes. The date range is inclusive. If the start or end date falls on a weekend or excluded bank holiday, that day is removed from the working-day total.

Which bank holiday years are included?

The built-in bank holiday sets cover 2026 and 2027 for England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Dates outside those years will still exclude weekends, but may not include every regional bank holiday.

Does the page upload my dates?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser with local JavaScript.
