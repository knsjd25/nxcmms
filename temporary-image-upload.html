What is a temporary image upload?

A temporary image upload creates a hosted link intended to expire after the retention period selected before upload. It is useful when an image only needs to remain available for a limited conversation, review or task.

How to use temporary image upload

Use the same working upload tool and select the temporary duration that matches the task.

Temporary links suit content that is useful for a defined period and does not need permanent hosting.

Bug reports

Draft reviews

Limitations, expiry and privacy

The selected image is uploaded to a remote service and may remain reachable until the chosen retention period ends. Expiry is based on the current service choices, not a promise that a private copy exists.

Use 1 day for quick previews, 7 days for short feedback, or 30 days for longer temporary references. Do not upload sensitive, confidential, illegal, adult, harmful or infringing content.

FAQ

What is a temporary image upload?

It is a hosted image link created with a selected short-term retention period.

How long does a temporary image stay online?

Choose 1 day, 7 days or 30 days before uploading.

Can I upload without signing up?

Yes. The normal upload flow does not require an account.

Can I share the link in messages or forums?

Yes, if the destination accepts the link and you have permission to share the image.

What formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, GIF and WebP images up to 5 MB are supported.

Can I delete an uploaded image?

Temporary images follow the selected retention setting. Removal requests can be sent with the image URL through the contact page.

Is temporary image upload free?

Yes. The public upload page provides the temporary retention options without account registration.
