Goal: complete your first successful upload

This guide covers only the first successful upload path. For expiry details see Temporary Image Upload. For Markdown, HTML or BBCode embedding see Share Image Link.

Worked example: from file to openable link

Use a non-sensitive JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP file of 5 MB or less that you are allowed to share.

Measured path on the upload page

  1. Open /upload and choose the image.
  2. Select a retention period (1, 7 or 30 days).
  3. Complete the verification question and start the upload.
  4. Copy the Direct URL from the result panel.
  5. Open that URL in a new browser tab to confirm the image loads.

If the first upload fails

Use the upload terminal messages on /upload. Typical fixes:

  • File larger than 5 MB

    Compress or resize the image, then choose it again.

  • Unsupported format

    Convert to JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP before uploading.

  • Verification error

    Refresh the question, enter the new result, then retry.

  • Service or network error

    Wait a moment and retry. Do not assume success if the result panel stays empty.

Short limits reminder

Hosted links are shareable, not private storage. Do not upload identity documents, private screenshots, financial files, confidential work, illegal content or images you cannot share. Retention and removal details are covered on the temporary-upload guide and the contact page.

Accepted: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP up to 5 MB. Removal or abuse reports: contact page or yuyananuu@gmail.com with the image URL.

FAQ

How do I know the first upload worked?

The result panel shows a Direct URL and the image opens in a new tab.

Do I need an account for the first upload?

No. Normal uploads on /upload do not require an account.

What if verification keeps failing?

Refresh the question and enter only the new result. Old answers will not match.
