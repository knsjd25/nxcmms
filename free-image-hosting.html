What is free image hosting?

Free image hosting stores an image on a remote service and gives you a URL that can be shared or embedded. Mini Tools sends the selected file to its image hosting service only after you choose it on the upload page.

The upload flow is designed for a quick link without account registration.

Limitations, privacy and public links

Image hosting is not local-only processing: the selected image is sent to the service so a link can be created. Anyone who receives that link may be able to open it.

Do not upload identity documents, private screenshots, financial files, confidential work, illegal content or images you do not have permission to share. Available retention options are 1 day, 7 days, 30 days and approved permanent storage.

FAQ

Is this image hosting tool free?

Yes. The public upload page can create hosted image links without an account.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required for the normal upload flow.

Can I use the image link on forums or blogs?

Yes, when the forum or website accepts direct URLs, Markdown, HTML or BBCode and you have permission to share the image.

What image formats are supported?

The upload page accepts JPG, PNG, GIF and WebP images.

Is there an upload size limit?

Yes. The current limit is 5 MB per image.

Are uploaded images public?

Hosted links should be treated as shareable rather than private. Do not upload sensitive material.
