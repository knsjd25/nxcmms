How this QR code generator helps

A QR code is a quick way to move from paper, packaging, signage or a screen into an action on a phone. This page supports common static QR formats: website links, plain text, Wi-Fi access, email actions and simple contact details.

The QR image is generated in your browser. The page does not need to send your QR content to Mini-Tools.uk to build the image.

Use the generator for quick sharing when a typed URL, Wi-Fi password, email address or contact card would be inconvenient. Before printing or publishing, scan the QR code with a phone to confirm the destination and details are correct.

Before printing or sharing

  • Scan the QR code with a phone before printing it on a poster or label.
  • Use higher error correction if the QR code may be printed small or placed near a logo.
  • For URLs, make sure the destination page will stay online after the QR code is distributed.
  • For Wi-Fi QR codes, check that the SSID and password are exactly correct.

Is this QR code generator private?

QR code generation happens locally in your browser. The page does not need to send QR content to Mini-Tools.uk to create the image.

Will the QR code expire?

The QR image itself does not expire. It continues to work as long as the encoded destination or content remains valid.

What does error correction do?

Higher error correction can make a QR code more resilient to damage or obstruction, but it also makes the code visually denser.

How to use this QR Code Generator

Use this QR generator for links, short text and quick sharing between devices. It is suited to simple QR codes where the source content is already safe to publish.

Use cases

Example: paste a landing-page URL, generate a QR code and download it for a poster or handout.

Limitations

QR codes expose the encoded content. Avoid secrets, private tokens and links that should not be widely shared.

FAQ

Can I change the destination after printing? No. Static QR codes keep the encoded text unless you use a redirect URL you control.
