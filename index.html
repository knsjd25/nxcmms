Frequently asked questions

Short answers that make the homepage useful for visitors and clearer for review.

Why is the homepage organised into three categories?

A small number of broad categories makes the tool directory easier to understand. More detailed subcategories can be added later inside category pages.

Why are Tax and Image Hosting the only popular tools?

They are the strongest current tools and should stay visible without overwhelming the homepage with every utility.

Does image hosting upload files to a server?

Yes. Hosted image links require server storage. The upload page should explain retention options, removal requests and prohibited content.

Are calculator results official advice?

No. Calculators provide estimates for planning and checking only. They do not replace professional, tax, legal or financial advice.
