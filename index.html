Frequently asked questions

Short answers for visitors who want to understand what the site does, how the calculators should be used and what privacy limits apply.

What is Mini-Tools.uk?

Mini-Tools.uk is a practical online tools website with calculators, developer utilities, image helpers, PDF conversion tools and everyday browser utilities.

Can I use the UK Tax Calculator as a UK income tax calculator?

Yes. The UK Tax Calculator estimates Income Tax, National Insurance, pension contributions, student loan repayments and take-home pay. It is useful for quick salary planning, but it is not formal tax advice.

Do image and PDF tools upload my files?

Some tools are designed to work in the browser, while hosted image upload features require a server connection. Check the privacy note on each tool page before using sensitive files.

Are the calculators official financial or tax advice?

No. Calculator pages are intended for estimates, learning and planning. For tax, legal, mortgage or contractor decisions, check official sources or speak with a qualified professional.

Is Mini-Tools.uk free to use?

Most tools can be opened and used directly without creating an account. Some upload or hosted-link features may include limits to keep the service stable.
