FAQ

What is Mini-Tools.uk focused on?

The homepage focuses on UK calculators for tax, salary, VAT, property and contractor planning, while keeping other useful tools available.

Are the UK calculator results official?

No. They are quick estimates for planning and education. For important financial decisions, check official guidance or speak to a qualified adviser.

Where are the developer and image tools?

They are in the Other useful tools section and can also be found through the search box.

Do I need an account?

Most tools can be opened and used without an account.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Yes. Use the Contact page to send feedback, report a problem or suggest a new UK calculator or browser tool.
