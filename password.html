How to use this password generator

Choose a length and character mix, generate a password, then save it in a trusted password manager. Longer unique passwords are usually more useful than short complex passwords reused across sites.

Use cases

Create unique credentials for new accounts, router settings or encrypted archives, then store them securely.

Limitations and privacy

This page cannot store, recover or audit your passwords. Do not paste generated passwords into chat, email or documents that other people can access.

FAQ

Should I reuse a generated password? No. Generate a unique password for each account.

Does this page store generated passwords? No. Generation happens in your browser and the page does not provide password storage.

What makes a strong password example? A strong password is long, random and unique. A 16-character mix of upper and lower case letters, numbers and symbols is harder to guess than a short reused password.
