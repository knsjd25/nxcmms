1. Browser-based tools

Many pages on Mini-Tools.uk are designed to run their main task in the browser. This can include tax and VAT calculations, JSON formatting, text comparison, token estimation, password generation, QR generation, image resizing or other lightweight utilities.

When a tool works this way, the content you enter for that tool is normally processed on your device rather than uploaded to a Mini-Tools.uk application server for the core operation.

  • Local processing means the main task happens in the browser.
  • It does not mean the whole website works offline or that no external assets are loaded.
  • A page may still load scripts, analytics, icons, images or other static assets.

2. Image hosting and uploaded files

The free image hosting page at /upload cannot work as a local-only tool. To create a hosted image URL, the selected image file and required upload settings are sent to a remote endpoint.

Uploaded images may be accessible to anyone who has the generated link. Do not upload private documents, identity documents, financial files, confidential work material, sensitive screenshots, illegal content, adult content, violent or hateful content, copyrighted content without permission, malware, phishing or scam-related content.

  • The selected image is uploaded so a hosted URL can be created.
  • The chosen retention period, optional long-term storage code and anti-bot verification data may be sent with the upload request.
  • Hosted links may remain available after you close the browser tab, depending on the selected retention option.
Important upload reminder: Only upload images that you are comfortable sharing through a public or semi-public link. If you need an uploaded image removed, contact us with the image URL and the reason for the request.

3. Image retention and deletion requests

The image hosting tool may offer retention choices such as 1 day, 7 days, 30 days or permanent storage when an approved long-term storage code is used. The selected option controls how the service is intended to handle the hosted image.

To request removal of an uploaded image, send the image URL, the removal reason and any relevant rights information to the contact email below. Mini-Tools.uk may remove hosted images that appear to violate upload rules, privacy rights, copyright rules, applicable law or service safety.

Removal and privacy requests can be sent to: yuyananuu@gmail.com

4. Analytics and basic website data

Mini-Tools.uk uses Google Analytics to understand broad site usage, such as which pages are visited, what devices or browsers are commonly used, and how visitors move around the site.

Analytics is separate from the content you may process inside a local browser tool. For example, visiting the tax calculator page and entering a salary amount for a browser-side calculation are different types of activity.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. Google provides its own privacy controls and policies for analytics-related data.

5. Advertising

Some pages on Mini-Tools.uk may display advertising. Advertising partners, including Google advertising services where used, may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure or manage ads according to their own policies.

Advertising does not change whether a tool is local-only or server-backed. Tool pages should still explain whether user input stays in the browser or is uploaded to a service.

6. Cookies, localStorage and browser storage

Some pages may use browser storage, such as localStorage, to remember preferences like language, tool settings, recent values or interface state.

Browser storage stays on your device unless a page explicitly sends something elsewhere as part of a remote feature. You can usually clear stored preferences through your browser site data settings.

7. Contact messages

If you contact Mini-Tools.uk by email, the information you send becomes part of that email conversation. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information or highly sensitive personal information through email.

Privacy questions can be sent to: yuyananuu@gmail.com

8. Third-party links and services

Mini-Tools.uk may link to third-party websites, documentation, libraries or services. Those websites and services have their own privacy practices. Mini-Tools.uk does not control third-party websites.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site adds, removes or rewrites tools. The aim is to keep the wording aligned with how the tools actually work.

Do Search Tools run locally?
